# LEDGER — the papers in running order

Chronological view of the SuperInstance-papers library: every digested paper in the order it arrived, grouped by year and month. Each entry carries the date, a link, the projects connected to the paper at its time, and a one-line summary. Dates are marked `(git)` when the evidence is the paper's first commit in this repo, and `(inferred)` when the evidence is a date in the filename or folder timestamp; unmarked dates are stated in the document itself. Papers with no date evidence at all sit under **Undated** at the end.

The 229 papers copied in during the library consolidation of 2026-10-28 carry that copy-in date in git but often state their own earlier writing date; where neither exists, they are listed as undated rather than guessed. A few stated dates read as mis-datings by their own context (two "October 2023" headers against 2025–26 siblings; two "January 2025" papers whose research-run summary was generated 2026-03-09) — they are kept as stated and flagged in `notes/forgotten-knowledge.md` §6. See `notes/library-reorganisation-2026-10-28.md` for the full date-evidence policy.
