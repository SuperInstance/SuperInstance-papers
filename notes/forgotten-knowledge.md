# Forgotten knowledge — the salvage notebook

Written 2026-10-28, during the full digest of the library (355 papers, every collection).
This notebook collects what only survives in these papers: old project names, dead and
renamed things, numbers and facts recorded nowhere else, claims that died, and programmes
announced but never run. Each entry cites the paper(s) it came from. Where I can tell, I
say plainly whether something looks **lost for good** or merely **dormant**. Quotes are
verbatim from the cited papers.
