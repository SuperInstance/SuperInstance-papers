# WP-02 — JEV: the Joint Embedding Validator

**Claim.** Understanding can live in *distribution*: a validator that is
both a non-parametric field (deformed by every judgment it makes) and a tiny
parametric head, grown from what actually flows through the system, beats
one-shot training on two counts — it cannot be separated from its use, and
its idle time is compile time.

## Mechanism

A JEV is *kinda both*:

- a **rhizome** — a non-parametric, non-collapsing field that deforms with
  every judgment. Judging is not free: the judge is changed by judging. The
  field is the memory of every call the quilt made of it.
- a **vessel head** — tiny deterministic trainers (no torch, no gradient
  stack) that distill the accumulated field into versioned artifacts.

The growth law: observe what the quilt actually judges (receipt rows,
escalations, teacher answers); grow **only where the wave collapses** —
explicit observations carry ground truth (the ExoJ law); everything else is
idle-time metabolism. The idle-time **weaver** compiles the accumulated
ExoJ into versioned **weave artifacts**, and the next inference starts from
the distilled weave instead of the raw field. Nothing is trained once:
every weave is versioned, receipted, and promoted or DISCARDed by a sealed
gate — a gardener's ledger, not a checkpoint zoo.

Three substrates (vector embedding, torch, neither) are grown on identical
soil and priced against each other rather than assumed — the bake-off is
the epistemology.

## Evidence

jev-garden receipts show the loop closing: judgment rows in, weave versions
out, promotions and DISCARDs both landing in the ledger. In madlibs-jev
(wave 67) the JEV-nudge instrument receipts its own deformation per pass
(`jevMem` grows, decays, never resets) — the rhizome law in ~20 lines.

## Bones

- The judgment-receipt schema: every evaluation carries its context, so the
  validator's history is replayable.
- The weave/promotion gate pattern: versioned artifacts + sealed promotion
  is reusable for any learned component, not just validators.
- Idle-time compilation as a budget: the system's quiet hours pay for its
  loud hours.

## Limits

Non-parametric fields grow monotonically; forgetting is scheduled, not
learned. The "wave collapse" growth heuristic needs explicit observations —
silent regions of the quilt contribute nothing. And a deforming judge is
harder to audit than a frozen one: the receipt chain is the only witness.
