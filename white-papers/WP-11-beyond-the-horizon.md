# WP-11 — Beyond the Horizon: what the receipts point at next

**Claim.** The fleet's wave-67 systems (madlibs-jev, purpose-loops,
unspoken-resonance) each carry an honest limit whose remedy already exists
in a sibling repo. The horizon is not new machinery — it is **bridges**:
quantized memory for deadbands, warm-started resonance, paradigm games over
cells, and a bone library shared across task shapes.

## Bridge 1 — Quantized exocortex memory (turbovec-substrate → WP-03)

turbovec-substrate indexes substrate cells with TurboQuant: data-oblivious
random rotation, **4-bit Lloyd-Max quantization, 8× compression verified**,
prev_hash chain intact per cell. The exocortex's deadbands currently
calibrate from variance of past nights held per-ExoJ. Indexing every past
beat as a quantized cell means the deadband can **search** instead of
average: "what did I do last time this trigger shape appeared, and how far
outside my band was it?" — shape-similar priors at 1/8 the memory, hash-
chained so the memory is itself a receipt chain.

## Bridge 2 — Warm-started resonance (jev-turbovec → WP-09)

jev-turbovec's pattern: *every diffusion is indexed; future diffusions find
similar past runs for inspiration.* Unspoken-resonance starts every
campaign from a cold field and spends passes finding agreement. Index every
tuned field as a turbovec cell (target = seed shape, combined = final
field), and the next campaign **warm-starts**: `find_similar()` returns the
nearest tuned drum, the chorus begins from that field, and the passes spent
drop — the cost curve of WP-08, applied to tuning itself.

## Bridge 3 — Paradigm games over cells (madlibs-gan-turbovec × madlibs-jev → WP-05)

madlibs-gan-turbovec already indexes past paradigm games. Gen-4 madlibs
sheets are typed cells. The obvious mating: a GAN game where the producer
emits **cell sheets** (not strings), the filler supplies `situation` cells,
the critic reads the *nudge field* rather than the prose, the edge-finder
hunts formulas that produce muffled drums (weight collapse — WP-09's guard
becomes a game objective), and JEV + JEPA vote on receipts, not vibes.
Every canonized winner is a template version, append-only, parent-chained —
the GAN game becomes a **template evolution engine with a memory**.

## Bridge 4 — The bone library (purpose-loops → WP-07/WP-08)

purpose-loops ships one task shape; its bone registry is shape-scoped. The
horizon: a **cross-shape bone library** — lexicons, validators, LUTs
indexed by the turbovec pattern so that a loop beginning a *new* shape can
ask "which existing bones fit this fixture?" The portfolio of purposes
(purpose-loops' stated limit) becomes concrete: purposes share a bone
economy, and the Jig Law stops being per-repo and becomes fleet
infrastructure.

## Bridge 5 — Playable papers

The demo pages (madlibs playground, loop visualizer, chorus player) taught
something the papers cannot: **a demo is a claim you can perturb**. The
madlibs demo literally lets a reader change the blanks and watch the run
evolve — that is the founder's "demonstrate like a madlibs" doctrine. The
horizon here is method, not machinery: every future white paper ships with
its playable twin, receipts bundled, no network required.

## What this paper leaves behind

A build order, not just a wish list: (1) turbovec index inside
madlibs-jev's shape memory (its stated stub), (2) resonance field
warm-starting (one `find_similar` call before pass 1), (3) the cell-sheet
GAN game (compose two engines, both receipted), (4) the bone library (one
new repo, or fleet-seeds grows a wing). Each bridge is a small PR against
an existing receipted system — jigs all the way down.

## Limits

All five bridges are unimplemented as of this paper — stated with receipts
of absence. The 8× compression claim is turbovec-substrate's, verified on
its own tests (9/9), not yet on fleet workloads. Warm-starting risks
homogenization: if every drum starts from a similar past field, the
quantum breath of the moth matters more, not less.
